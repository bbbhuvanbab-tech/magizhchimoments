# Premium Event Planning Homepage

## What will change
- Reposition the opening message around complete premium event planning and management, while retaining the existing logo, imagery, typography, animations, and black-and-gold styling.
- Update the navigation to **Home, Services, Events, Portfolio, About, Contact** and make **Plan Your Event** the prominent gold action.
- Add a refined **Services** section covering planning, design, venue/vendor coordination, guest experience, production, and on-day management.
- Add an **Events** section presenting weddings, engagements, birthdays, baby showers, corporate events, and private celebrations.
- Refine supporting homepage copy and calls-to-action so the business reads as an end-to-end event partner rather than decoration-only.
- Keep all existing portfolio photos and category showcases intact.

## Navigation behavior
- Services and Events will link directly to their new homepage sections.
- Portfolio, About, and Contact will keep their existing pages.
- Plan Your Event will open the existing enquiry page.

## Technical details
- Frontend-only edits to the homepage and navigation.
- Use existing semantic black/gold design tokens and established responsive patterns.
- No changes to Lovable Cloud, enquiries, admin access, authentication, domain settings, or portfolio data.
- Verify desktop and mobile navigation, section links, and the enquiry call-to-action in the live preview.
